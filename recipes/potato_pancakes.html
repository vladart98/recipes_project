Potato Pancakes

Description

Potato pancakes are crispy on the outside and tender on the inside. Made with grated potatoes, onions, and a few simple ingredients.

Total time: 15 mins

Ingridients

  • 4 large potatoes
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • salt to taste
  • black pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the potatoes

    Grate the potatoes and squeeze out as much excess liquid as possible.

  2. Mix the ingridients

    In a large bowl, combine the grated potatoes, onion, egg, flour, salt, and pepper. Mix well.

  3. Heat the oil

    Pour the oil into a frying pan and heat it over medium heat.

  4. Cook the pancakes

    Spoon portions of the mixture into the pan and flatten them slightly. Fry for 3–4 minutes on each side until golden brown and crispy.

  5. Drain excess oil

    Transfer the cooked pancakes to a plate lined with paper towels.

Bon appetit!
